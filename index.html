Reference

8F7Q, active-state human MOR with β-endorphin bound. Chain P residues 1–5 (YGGFM) are the experimental met-enkephalin pose. Released 2022-12-14, after the Protenix base_default_v1.0.0 training cutoff of 2021-09-30, so this complex was not in training. DAMGO's 6DDF was, so the YGGF-in-pocket motif was learnable.

Two things this figure does not show

Every prediction over-tightens the salt bridge

The experimental Tyr1 amine sits 4.55 Å from Asp149. All three predictions place it closer — 1.11, 2.45 and 2.55 Å. These are energy-minimised or model-confident contacts, not measured physiological distances, and should not be quoted as such.

Recovering one pose is not a screen

Met-enkephalin has a crystal structure to check against; a milk-derived candidate does not. Scoring on this pilot tracks global burial and peptide length rather than the D3.32 register: the best mis-registered HADDOCK pose scored −70.0 and outranked 9 of 10 near-native poses, and co-folding confidence reaches AUROC 0.500 once length is controlled.
